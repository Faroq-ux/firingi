# Food Safety (HACCP) Logbook (Phase 69)

A food safety logbook for customers and wholesalers on FIRINGI. It covers temperatures, checklists, cleaning, delivery
checks, cooling and training, and produces a report ready for an inspector.

## Customer pages (25 languages)

| Page | What |
| --- | --- |
| `/buy/food-safety` — Today's Checks | Each piece of equipment with its check times. A time is **Done**, **Out of range**, **Due now**, **Late** (time + FIRINGI's grace has passed) or **Later**. The page also has today's checklists and cleaning, cooling, issues from the last 7 days, and completion today and over 7 days. It also shows tips. |
| `/buy/food-safety/log` — Logbook & Reports | Every record with its corrective action. Filters: period, kind, only problems. Shows completion per day and missed checks. **Download report (PDF)** covers up to 3 months. |
| `/buy/food-safety/deliveries` — Delivery Checks | FIRINGI deliveries from the last 7 days to check on arrival, plus deliveries from other suppliers. The check covers temperature (chilled / frozen limits), intact packaging and use-by dates. A failed FIRINGI delivery offers **Open a claim** (`/buy/orders/<id>/claim`). |
| `/buy/food-safety/training` — Food Safety Training | Who did which course, the certificate number and the valid-until date. A warning comes before it runs out. |
| `/buy/food-safety/setup` — Logbook Setup (`buyer.haccp.manage`) | Equipment: fridge, freezer, cold room, hot holding, display. Each has its limits and up to 6 check times. Checklists and cleaning run at opening, at closing, daily, weekly or monthly, each with items and optionally a photo. Templates: **Restaurant**, **Café / bakery**, **Shop / butcher**, with names in the business's language. |

Anything out of range or not OK needs **the corrective action taken** before it saves. Staff can take back their own
entries the same day; a manager can take back any entry, and the audit trail keeps a record of it.

Permissions:
- `buyer.haccp.manage`: setup, training, and taking back any entry.
- `buyer.haccp.log`: recording.

Roles:
- Kitchen Manager: manage + log.
- Purchaser, Cashier and Shop Manager: log.
- Company admins: everything.

## FIRINGI (`/admin/food-safety`, English; Risk & Compliance › Food Safety)

- **Overview:**
  - businesses set up and recording
  - average completion over 7 days
  - records and out-of-range readings over 7 days
  - expired training
  - businesses below 80 %
- **Businesses:** per business, its equipment, checklists, records, completion, out-of-range readings and last record, plus its own switch.
- **Settings:**
  - on / off
  - who uses the logbook (customers, wholesalers)
  - parts: temperatures, checklists, cleaning, delivery checks, cooling, training, photos, reports, reminders, AI tips
  - which templates are offered
  - rules: cooling target and time, minutes before a check counts as late, the training warning, delivery limits
  - per business: Always on / Off

## Job (hourly, `haccp`)

- Late temperature checks: one notification per business per day (`haccp.checks_missed`).
- Training running out: once per record (`haccp.training_expiring`).
- An out-of-range reading or failed checklist notifies managers at once (`haccp.out_of_range`).

## AI

- Tips on Today's Checks:
  - equipment drifting towards its limit, or out of range 3 or more times in a week
  - checks missed often
  - deliveries not checked
  - training running out or expired
- The customer's Assistant has the tool `my_food_safety`. It reports completion, what is still due today, issues and tips.

## Report (PDF)

Portuguese with English, generated by `src/server/pdf/haccp.tsx` from `/api/haccp/report?from=&to=`. It contains:
- completion
- every temperature reading against its limit, with who recorded it and the corrective action
- checklists and cleaning
- deliveries received
- cooling
- missed checks
- training
